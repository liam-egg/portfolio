About Me 

Hello! I'm Liam Eggleston, a 16-year-old high school junior living in Austin, Texas. Being born on the coast of California, I've always been concerned with the gradual deterioration of many of the most beautiful natural parts of the Earth. To combat this, I've decided to do as much as I can to spread awareness about our climate with the resources I have; hence was born climology.

At school, I'm fascinated by STEM: I am currently taking Astrophysics, Multivariable Calculus, and working on computer and data science projects on my own time. My latest project involves handwriting simulation with the long-term goal of helping the blind learn to write, a vital tool those with sight take for granted.

In the rest of my free time, I like to climb rocks. I've been climbing competitively for a few years now. This past summer I completed a voyage to Norway, where I climbed among the untamed fjords at Hanshelleren Cave. Climbing has only immersed me further in nature, and I hate to see our Earth wither away.
