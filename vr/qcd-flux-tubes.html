A model, not a lattice simulation: the energy density of a Gaussian flux tube carrying the string tension σ = 0.9 GeV/fm, its width growing as ln r (Lüscher), plus each quark's short-range colour-Coulomb field. The pair energy is the Cornell potential −κ/r + σr; past 1.25 fm making a new quark–antiquark pair is cheaper than more string, so it breaks. QED draws the same pair's electric dipole field, on a scale twelve times more sensitive so its spread-out field shows.
