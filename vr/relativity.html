Light here crawls at 20 m/s, so the effects of near-light speed are what you see. Each vertex is drawn where its light appears to come from in your moving frame (aberration); surfaces reflect a 5,800 K sun as real spectra, shifted by the Doppler factor and turned back into colour through the CIE 1931 eye response; brightness scales as D⁵. Stars are blackbodies, seen at D·T.
