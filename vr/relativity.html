Light here crawls at 20 m/s, so the effects of near-light speed are what you see. Aberration only changes the direction light arrives from, so every pixel looks its ray up, through the exact Lorentz transformation, in the world as the station sees it from your eye. Surfaces reflect a 5,800 K sun as real spectra, shifted by the Doppler factor and turned back into colour through the CIE 1931 eye response; brightness scales as D⁵. Stars are blackbodies, seen at D·T.
