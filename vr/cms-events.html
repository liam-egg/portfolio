Real CMS collisions recorded in 2011 at √s = 7 TeV (CMS HEP Tutorial ntuple, CERN Open Data). It holds reconstructed objects, not hits: muons are propagated through an approximate 3.8 T solenoid and −1.8 T return-yoke field, and the chambers they cross are worked out from a simplified CMS layout.
